8. WHEN TO CHANGE PARTITIONS
----------------------------
Increase partitions when:
- Dataset is large
- Partitions are too few
- Some tasks are processing too much data
- More parallelism is needed

Decrease partitions when:
- Dataset becomes smaller
- Too many small partitions exist
- Reducing task scheduling overhead is useful

9. REPARTITION VS COALESCE
--------------------------
repartition(n): Can increase or decrease partitions.
                 Uses a shuffle.
coalesce(n):    Mainly decreases partitions.
                 Usually avoids a full shuffle.
